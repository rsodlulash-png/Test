ABOUT IMFUNDO QUEST

Education opens possibilities.

Imfundo Quest is a student learning platform created to make practising school subjects more engaging and accessible. It uses short learning activities, quizzes and saved progress to encourage consistent learning.

The platform is intended to support CAPS learners, not replace teachers, textbooks or the official curriculum. Lesson content should be checked against current CAPS requirements by qualified educators.
